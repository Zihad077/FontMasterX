50+ Decorative Text Symbols and Borders for Social Media

Design · 5 min read · Sep 19, 2026

Sometimes a single symbol can make all the difference. A decorative border around your name, a small sparkle next to your tagline, or a divider between sections — these tiny details make your social media profiles look polished and professional.

Why Decorative Text Symbols Matter

Social media platforms like Instagram, TikTok, and Discord give you very little control over formatting. You cannot add borders or dividers with a button. But with Unicode decorative symbols, you can copy and paste these elements into any text field.
